# KB02: My printer shows "offline"

**Who this is for:** any user who cannot print. No technical knowledge needed.

## Try these steps first
1. **Look at the printer.** Is it on? Does its screen show an error such as a paper jam, no paper or low toner? Fix that first.
2. **Check the connection.** If it uses a cable, make sure both ends are plugged in. If it is wireless, make sure your computer is on the office network, not the guest network.
3. **Restart the printer.** Turn it off, wait one minute, and turn it back on.
4. **Clear stuck print jobs.** On your computer, open Printers settings, open this printer's queue, and cancel any stuck jobs.
5. **Print a test page** from Printers settings.

## Still offline? Contact the help desk with:
- The printer's name and location
- The error message on the printer or your computer
- **Whether other people can print to it.** If nobody can, tell us right away: that affects the whole team and is handled with higher priority.

**For support staff:** see ticket T-003 in the [ticket log](../03_help_desk_tickets/ticket_log.md). If several users are affected, treat it as a wider outage and escalate.
