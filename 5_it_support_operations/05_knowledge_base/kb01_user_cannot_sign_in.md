# KB01: I can't sign in to my computer

**Who this is for:** any user who cannot sign in. No technical knowledge needed.

## Try these steps first
1. **Check Caps Lock.** Passwords are case-sensitive. Turn Caps Lock off and try again.
2. **Check your username.** Make sure it is spelled correctly and that you are signing in with your work account.
3. **Stop after a few tries.** Too many wrong passwords can lock your account. If you see "account locked," wait and do not keep trying.
4. **Expired password.** If you are asked to change your password, follow the prompts and choose a new one you have not used before.
5. **Restart the computer** and try once more.

## Keep your account safe
- Never share your password with anyone, including the help desk. We will never ask for it.

## Still can't sign in? Contact the help desk with:
- Your name and username
- Your computer name (on a label or in the sign-in screen)
- The exact error message (a photo with your phone is fine)
- When the problem started

**For support staff:** verify the user's identity before unlocking or resetting an account. See ticket T-001 in the [ticket log](../03_help_desk_tickets/ticket_log.md).
